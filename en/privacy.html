Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

CockpitView has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not run any server and receives no data from the app.

What the app uses on your device

Location: your flight's speed, track, altitude and position on the navigation display. Used only while the app is open.

Motion and barometer: pitch, roll, heading and vertical speed.

Settings (units, display style, calibration) stay on your device.

What leaves your device, and where it goes

To provide some features, the app sends requests directly from your device to these services. The requests carry no name, account or device identifier.

Flight lookup (plane mode)

Sent to: adsbdb.com and hexdb.io, public flight-route APIs

What is sent: The flight's callsign, e.g. DLH400

INIT REQUEST (plane mode, optional)

Sent to: simbrief.com

What is sent: The SimBrief username you entered, to fetch your latest SimBrief plan

Offline flight download (plane mode)

Sent to: Amazon Web Services, Open Data terrain tiles

What is sent: Requests for the map tiles along the flight's route

Speed limit (car and bike, can be switched off in the menu)

Sent to: OpenStreetMap Overpass API (overpass-api.de)

What is sent: Your current position, about once every 350 m

Route height profile (car and bike)

Sent to: Amazon Web Services, Open Data terrain tiles (s3.amazonaws.com)

What is sent: Requests for the map tiles that cover the planned route

Destination search and routing (car and bike)

Sent to: Apple Maps

What is sent: Your search text, position and destination

"Open in Google Maps" (car and bike, only when you tap it)

Sent to: Google Maps

What is sent: Your position and destination

Downloaded flights are stored only on your device and are deleted a week after the flight's date.

Each service handles these requests under its own privacy policy: OpenStreetMap Foundation, Amazon Web Services, Navigraph (SimBrief), Apple, Google.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
